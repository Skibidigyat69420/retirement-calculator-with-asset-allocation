## 2. Purpose of the IPS

This Investment Policy Statement is established to:

1. Define the investment objectives and constraints of the client.
2. Set clear guidelines for portfolio construction, implementation, and monitoring.
3. Establish roles, responsibilities, and communication protocols.
4. Provide a disciplined framework for rebalancing and tactical decisions.
5. Serve as the benchmark against which portfolio performance and suitability are evaluated.

---

## 3. Duties and Responsibilities

### 3.1 Client Responsibilities
- Provide accurate and timely financial, tax, and personal information.
- Communicate material changes in circumstances, goals, or constraints.
- Review account statements and performance reports.
- Approve material deviations from the IPS if requested.

### 3.2 Advisor Responsibilities
- Act in the client's best interest (fiduciary duty).
- Construct and manage the portfolio in accordance with this IPS.
- Provide regular reporting on portfolio holdings, performance, and risk.
- Maintain confidentiality and comply with applicable laws and regulations.
- Disclose conflicts of interest, fees, and compensation.

### 3.3 Investment Manager Responsibilities (if applicable)
- Execute investment decisions within the mandate defined by this IPS.
- Report investment activity, attribution, and risk metrics.
- Maintain investment process documentation.
