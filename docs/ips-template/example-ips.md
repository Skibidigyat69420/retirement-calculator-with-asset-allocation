# Investment Policy Statement — Example

**Client Name:** Mr. Rajesh Mehta  
**Prepared By:** Sound Thesis Wealth Advisory  
**Date Prepared:** 28 August 2026  
**Next Review Date:** 28 August 2027  
**Client Type:** Individual / HNI

---

## 1. Client Description & Background

Rajesh Mehta, aged 45, is a Mumbai-based technology entrepreneur. He is married with two children (ages 12 and 15). His primary source of wealth is a 60% stake in a privately held SaaS business. He has recently sold a minority stake, generating INR 15 crore in investable liquidity.

- **Approximate Net Worth:** INR 42 crore
- **Annual Income:** INR 3.2 crore (salary + dividends)
- **Liabilities:** INR 1.8 crore home loan
- **Investment Experience:** Moderate; familiar with Indian equities and mutual funds, limited exposure to fixed income and alternatives.

---

## 2. Purpose of the IPS

This IPS governs the management of Rajesh's INR 15 crore investable portfolio. The portfolio is intended to fund retirement, children's higher education, and build a liquid wealth reserve outside the concentrated business holding.

---

## 3. Duties and Responsibilities

- **Client:** Provide accurate financial information and approve material strategy changes.
- **Advisor (Sound Thesis):** Construct and manage the portfolio, provide quarterly reports, and rebalance as per policy.
- **Tax Consultant:** Coordinate tax-loss harvesting and capital-gains planning.

---

## 4. Investment Objectives

### 4.1 Return Objective
- **Primary goal:** Capital appreciation with inflation protection.
- **Nominal annual return target:** 11%–12%
- **Real annual return target:** 6%–7% (after 5% inflation)
- **Required return to meet retirement goal:** 10.5% p.a.
- **Benchmark:** NIFTY 500 Total Return Index for equity portion; NIFTY G-Sec index for debt portion.

### 4.2 Risk Objective
- **Risk tolerance:** Moderately Aggressive
- **Maximum acceptable drawdown:** -25%
- **Maximum annual volatility:** 14%
- **Minimum defensive allocation:** 25%

Rajesh has a stable income and a long time horizon, giving him high risk capacity. However, his concentrated business stake introduces correlated risk, so public equity exposure is capped.

---

## 5. Investment Constraints

### 5.1 Liquidity
- Emergency reserve: INR 75 lakh (6 months of expenses)
- Anticipated cash need: INR 50 lakh in 3 years for elder child's overseas education.

### 5.2 Time Horizon
- **Primary horizon:** 20 years (retirement at 65)
- **Stage:** Accumulation with a near-term liquidity need in 3 years.

### 5.3 Tax
- Resident Indian, highest tax slab.
- Long-term capital gains (LTCG) on equity: 12.5% beyond INR 1.25 lakh exemption.
- Tax-aware rebalancing is required.

### 5.4 Legal & Regulatory
- SEBI-registered investment advisor relationship.
- No leveraged positions.
- No speculative derivatives.
- Avoid sectors competing with the client's business (enterprise SaaS) to reduce conflict.

### 5.5 Unique Circumstances
- Highly concentrated exposure to private technology business.
- Desire to build liquid, diversified wealth outside the business.
- Plans to set up a family trust within 5 years.

---

## 6. Investment Guidelines

### Permitted
- Indian large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap equities via direct stocks, mutual funds, and ETFs.
- Government securities, corporate bond funds, and FD-like products.
- Gold ETFs and sovereign gold bonds.
- Real estate investment trusts (REITs) up to 5%.
- International equity index funds up to 10%.

### Prohibited
- Cryptocurrency.
- Speculative derivatives.
- Unlisted / unregulated schemes.
- Single-stock concentration > 8% of portfolio.

---

## 7. Strategic Asset Allocation

| Asset Class | Target % | Range | Expected Return | Expected Volatility |
|-------------|----------|-------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Equity — Large Cap | 35% | 30–45% | 12% | 15% |
| Equity — Mid/Small Cap | 15% | 10–20% | 14% | 22% |
| International Equity | 10% | 5–15% | 10% | 16% |
| Government Bonds | 20% | 15–25% | 7% | 5% |
| Corporate Bonds | 10% | 5–15% | 8% | 6% |
| Gold | 5% | 3–8% | 8% | 18% |
| Liquid / Cash | 5% | 3–10% | 6% | 1% |
| **Total** | **100%** | | **~10.8%** | **~12%** |

**Rationale:** The equity-heavy allocation reflects the long horizon and return target, while the 35% fixed income + gold allocation provides stability and liquidity for the education goal.

---

## 8. Rebalancing Policy

- **Method:** Threshold-based with annual calendar review.
- **Drift threshold:** ± 5% from target allocation.
- **Tax-aware:** Prefer in-kind switches and use of fresh contributions to rebalance toward underweight assets.
- **Emergency reserve:** Maintained in liquid funds; not subject to rebalancing.

---

## 9. Performance Measurement

- **Reporting:** Quarterly statements with portfolio valuation, returns, and asset allocation drift.
- **Primary metrics:** Absolute return, return vs. blended benchmark, Sharpe ratio, maximum drawdown.
- **Review meetings:** Semi-annual.

---

## 10. Monitoring & Review

- Annual IPS review or earlier if:
  - Sale of additional business stake
  - Change in employment or income
  - Marriage / education / health events
  - Significant market drawdown (>20%)
