# CFAI-Compliant Investment Policy Statement (IPS) Template

This directory contains a professional Investment Policy Statement (IPS) template structured according to CFA Institute (CFAI) standards for individual and institutional investors.

## What is an IPS?

An Investment Policy Statement is the governing document that defines the parameters within which a portfolio is managed. It aligns client objectives, constraints, and guidelines with investment strategy and serves as the foundation for fiduciary oversight.

## Files

- **`CFA_IPS_Template.md`** — Blank CFAI-compliant template ready to be filled out for any client.
- **`example-ips.md`** — A completed example for a hypothetical Indian HNI client.
- **`checklist.md`** — Quick checklist for advisors to ensure nothing is missed.

## CFA Institute IPS Structure

1. Client Description & Background
2. Purpose of the IPS
3. Duties and Responsibilities
4. Investment Objectives
   - Return objective
   - Risk tolerance
5. Investment Constraints
   - Liquidity
   - Time horizon
   - Tax considerations
   - Legal and regulatory
   - Unique circumstances (LLLRTU)
6. Investment Guidelines
7. Strategic Asset Allocation (SAA)
8. Rebalancing Policy
9. Monitoring, Review & Reporting
10. Appendices

## How to Use

1. Copy `CFA_IPS_Template.md` for each new client.
2. Conduct a thorough discovery interview covering risk capacity, goals, liabilities, cash-flow needs, and behavioral tendencies.
3. Fill in each section with client-specific data.
4. Document the strategic asset allocation and rebalancing rules.
5. Review and update at least annually or after any material change in client circumstances.
